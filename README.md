# CCL Products (India) Limited — Trading Comps Valuation

Comparable-company valuation of **CCL Products (India) Limited**, prepared as a financial-modelling portfolio project.

## Project overview

- **Valuation date:** 1 August 2025
- **Forecast period:** FY2026E–FY2027E
- **Currency:** INR crore, unless stated otherwise
- **Methods:** EV/EBITDA and P/E
- **Periods analysed:** LTM, FY2026E and FY2027E
- **Market price on valuation date:** ₹894.10 per share

## Selected peers

- Galaxy Surfactants Limited
- Fine Organic Industries Limited
- Syrma SGS Technology Limited
- Neuland Laboratories Limited
- Tata Consumer Products Limited

The peer set was selected by comparing business model, customer base, export exposure, product characteristics, growth, margins, size and leverage. Because no company is a perfect pure-play comparable, the report explains the relevance and limitations of each peer.

## Valuation output

Median peer multiples produce implied share values ranging from approximately **₹1,138 to ₹1,811** across the six valuation scenarios. The complete minimum-to-maximum output range is approximately **₹653 to ₹1,990 per share**.

The wide range reflects differences in peer scale, sector, profitability, growth and capital structure. The results should therefore be interpreted as a market-based valuation range rather than a precise target price.

## Data and methodology

The analysis uses publicly available annual reports, market information and broker-derived consensus estimates available on or before the valuation date. LTM figures are based on reported financials, while FY2026E and FY2027E figures use forward estimates. Enterprise value, normalized earnings and diluted shares were reviewed separately for CCL and each peer.
